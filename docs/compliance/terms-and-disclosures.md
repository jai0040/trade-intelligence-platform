## 1. Terms of Service (SaaS Agreement)

### 1.1 Nature of the Service
The AI Trading Intelligence SaaS Platform ("the Platform") provides computational algorithms, statistical market analytics, automated pattern detection, and algorithmic trade setup reference sheets ("Signals"). 

### 1.2 Absolute Signals-Only Policy
The Platform is strictly a **Signals-Only Information System**. The Platform:
1. **Never** holds client deposits, margin funds, securities, or cash.
2. **Never** connects directly to live broker execution APIs or routes electronic orders.
3. **Never** places, modifies, or cancels live market orders on behalf of any user.
All trade tickets, stop-loss calculations, and target levels generated by the Platform are read-only reference sheets intended solely for informational, analytical, and educational purposes.

### 1.3 User Responsibility & Risk Acknowledgment
Users agree that any trade, transaction, or financial decision made in reliance upon Platform outputs is taken at the user's sole, independent discretion and risk. The Platform and its operators shall not be liable for any trading losses, missed opportunities, or damages arising from market volatility.

## 3. Regulatory Posture — India (SEBI Compliance)

### 3.1 Technology Provider Status
The Platform operates as an algorithmic technology and data processing software service. It is **not** registered as an Investment Adviser under the SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013, nor as a Portfolio Manager under the SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations, 2020.

### 3.2 Automated Quantitative Models
All signal recommendations (Opening Range Breakout, Daily High/Low breakouts, Moving Average crosses) are computed algorithmically based on published technical market criteria without subjective human intervention or personalized tailoring.

### 3.3 Position Sizing Calculator
The platform position-sizing tool is a deterministic mathematical calculator. It operates purely on user-specified account sizes and user-defined risk percentages (e.g., 1% or 2%) and does not prescribe personalized investment limits.

## 4. Regulatory Posture — United States (SEC & FINRA Compliance)

### 4.1 Investment Advisers Act of 1940 (Publisher's Exclusion)
Under Section 202(a)(11)(D) of the Investment Advisers Act of 1940 and the United States Supreme Court precedent *Lowe v. SEC*, 472 U.S. 181 (1985), the Platform qualifies for the bona fide publisher's exclusion:
- The Platform publishes impersonal, algorithmic market commentary and computational data.
- The Platform does not provide personalized investment advice tailored to individual financial situations.
- The Platform maintains a regular, continuous publication schedule across all subscribers equally.

### 4.2 Securities Exchange Act of 1934
The Platform is not a registered broker-dealer under Section 15 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 and does not engage in underwriting, custody, or order routing.

### 4.3 STOCK Act of 2012 (Congressional Disclosures)
Congressional Periodic Transaction Reports (PTRs) displayed on the Platform are public records sourced from the US House Clerk. Subscribers are formally notified that statutory disclosure rules allow up to a **45-day filing lag** from the transaction date to the public filing date.

## 6. Privacy & Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR)

1. **Strict Tenant Isolation:** User trading journals, watchlists, preferences, and notifications are isolated via database row-level user ID constraints (`WHERE user_id = :current_user_id`).
2. **Password Security:** Credentials hashed exclusively using Argon2id (`time_cost=3`, `64 MiB`, `parallelism=2`). Plaintext passwords are never stored, transmitted, or logged.
3. **Right to Erasure & Export:** Users may request complete account deactivation and journal export in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and the General Data Protection Regulation (EU).
